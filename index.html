// WHAT IS LOOTSHRINE

A ROGUELITE BUILT
AROUND LOOT CHOICES

LootShrine puts loot decisions at the center of every run. Every item you find can be equipped for instant power, absorbed for temporary buffs mid-battle, or sacrificed at the Shrine for permanent stat gains that last the run.

One-handed portrait play. Turn-based pacing you control. Deep item synergies through set bonuses. Every floor a new puzzle to solve. Guardian bosses every 10 floors — The Origin at Floor 100, and past that, The Unwritten awaits at Floor 120.

// THE LOOP

HOW A RUN WORKS

Every floor follows the same rhythm — but each decision changes everything.

01

BATTLE

Face waves of enemies in front and back rows. Manage the in-battle shop to gear up mid-fight. Survive.

02

SHOP

Buy, absorb for temp stats, or let it pass. Reroll the shop — costs escalate per roll.

03

SHRINE

Sacrifice items for permanent stat gains. Donate gold. Pray for divine favor. Build devotion.

04

ASCEND

Climb higher through the tower. Enemies scale. New zones emerge. Guardian bosses every 10 floors.

05

SOUL FORGE

Run over? Spend earned souls on permanent upgrades. Grow stronger across every attempt.

// CORE MECHANIC

EVERY ITEM IS A
THREE-WAY DECISION

This single mechanic is the heart of LootShrine. No two runs feel the same.

01 / 03

EQUIP

Slot the item into your build for immediate stat bonuses. Complete sets for powerful set bonuses that transform your playstyle.

02 / 03

ABSORB

Consume the item for a temporary stat surge this battle. No turn cost — burn it for burst power when you need an edge right now.

03 / 03

SACRIFICE

Feed the shrine altar. Gain a fraction of the item's stats permanently for this run. Higher-rarity sacrifices yield greater gains. Critical sacrifices double the reward.

// THE META LAYER

THE AUTHOR’S WORD

A permanent meta system that carries across every future run. Compose your Word from Fragments you collect past the Origin — sockets, Ink, and real build decisions.

37 FRAGMENTS

GLYPHS — RAW STATLINES

Attack, Max HP, crit, defense, gold find, lifesteal, thorns. Straight, honest stat gains that stack the way you’d expect.

VERBS — TRIGGERED RULES

Rules that fire mid-combat. Silence the enemy on turn one, delete a target outright, cheat death once per run, auto-win a whole floor. Every Verb rewrites how a battle plays.

CLAUSES — GRAFTED ABILITIES

Abilities lifted from other heroes and slotted into yours. Run the Monk’s combo on a Knight. Summon a companion as a Mage. Class boundaries stop being walls.

SOCKETS COST INK

Sockets are limited and every Fragment costs Ink, so your Word is a real build decision. Merge duplicates to raise a Fragment’s tier, or salvage them for Souls. More sockets, deeper Ink, and rarer drops are earned in the Soul Forge’s new Words tab.

// THE ENDLESS MODE

ETERNAL CLIMB

An endless, escalating descent where you choose how hard your own run gets. No floor 100 finish line — only how far you can push before the dungeon finally wins.

UNLOCKED AT FLOOR 11+

YOU SET THE STAKES

Every two floors the shrine offers you three curses — Enraged, Vampiric, Fortified, regen auras, double-strikers, and more. You pick one. It binds to your run permanently and applies to every enemy from then on. Curses stack.

RISK = REWARD

Each run pays out Ancient Coins, the mode’s own currency, scaled to how deep you climbed and how many curses you bound. Take the nastier draft and climb richer, or play it safe and bank smaller — no correct answer, just how much pressure you’ll carry.

TEN WARDENS, SHUFFLED

Every tenth floor pits you against one of ten unique two-phase Wardens — Khoros the Ouroboros Warden, Mavros the Veil-Stitcher, Selene the Lantern of Names, the Eye Beyond the Climb, and six more. The order shuffles each run, so no two climbs feel the same.

CLIMB FOR THE LEADERBOARDS

Best Eternal depth and lifetime Ancient Coins both feed dedicated Game Center leaderboards — global and per character class. A clean, comparable measure of how good your runs really are. Spend coins on the Chests meta-progression tab to make the next climb a little stronger.

BUILT FOR DEPTH

TURN-BASED COMBAT

No time pressure. Think through every action. Attack, defend, use skills, or manage your in-battle shop.

ROW-BASED ENEMIES

Up to 6 enemies in front/back rows. Front row falls, back row promotes. Target priority matters.

SET BONUSES

Collect matching items to activate set bonuses. Each set rewards a different playstyle with unique effects.

SKILL TREES

Unlock nodes each run, reset on death. Class-specific trees define your combat style each attempt.

DIVINE ITEMS

Ultra-rare boss-only divine loot with unique mechanics that break the rules. Cannot be sacrificed — only equipped.

MERCHANT SYSTEM

The Shadow Peddler follows you through the tower, leveling up across runs with quests and rare artifacts.

Merge two same-rarity items to create a higher tier. Climb from Common all the way through Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, and Cosmic. Only Divine, Cursed, and Set items cannot be merged.

// COMMON QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Everything you might want to know before you tap Get on the App Store.

IS LOOTSHRINE FREE?

Yes. Download from the App Store and play the first 10 floors free, with no signup, no ads, and no time limit. If you defeat the floor 10 boss and want to keep climbing, the full 120-floor game unlocks with a single one-time purchase.

WHAT'S INCLUDED IN THE FREE 10 FLOORS?

A complete first chapter: the Knight class, the full combat and shrine systems, the merchant Grimwald, item collection and crafting, full UI in your language, and the floor 10 Gatekeeper boss fight.

ARE THERE ADS OR IN-APP PURCHASES?

No ads, ever. The main in-app purchase is the one-time unlock for floors 11–120. There are also optional cosmetic-only Supporter Packs — supporter portraits and frames that never affect gameplay, stats, or balance. The vast majority of portraits and frames are still earned for free through achievements and unlocks. No subscriptions, no loot boxes, and nothing pay-to-win.

DOES LOOTSHRINE WORK OFFLINE?

Yes. After the initial download, the entire game runs fully offline. Saves stay on your device and sync to your iCloud account when you’re online.

DOES IT SYNC BETWEEN IPHONE AND IPAD?

Yes. Saves sync via iCloud (Apple CloudKit), so you can continue your run on iPhone or iPad with the same progress.

WHAT DEVICES AND iOS VERSIONS ARE SUPPORTED?

LootShrine runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later.

DOES LOOTSHRINE SUPPORT GAME CENTER?

Yes. Global and per-class leaderboards plus achievements are available via Apple Game Center. Race friends and the world for the highest floor, most enemies slain, and fastest runs.

WHAT LANGUAGES DOES LOOTSHRINE SUPPORT?

Ten languages: English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Simplified Chinese, and Spanish. The game uses your device’s language automatically and lets you switch in-game.
