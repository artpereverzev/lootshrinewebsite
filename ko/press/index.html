한 줄 소개

LootShrine은 무료로 체험할 수 있는 iOS용 픽셀 아트 로그라이트 RPG로, 모든 아이템이 세 갈래의 선택이며 난이도는 여러분이 직접 정합니다.

짧은 소개 (~50단어)

1인 개발 iOS 픽셀 아트 로그라이트. 다크 판타지 타워를 오르며 모든 아이템을 장착하거나, 흡수해 일시적 버프를 얻거나, 신전에 바쳐 영구적인 힘을 얻습니다. 10개 층은 무료 체험, 한 번의 구매로 나머지가 열립니다. 광고 없음, 구독 없음, 페이투윈 없음.

중간 소개 (~120단어)

LootShrine is a turn-based, portrait-orientation roguelite RPG for iPhone and iPad, built solo by Tim Pereverzev. The hook is the three-way loot decision: every item you pick up can be equipped for stats, absorbed for a one-battle buff, or sacrificed at the shrine for permanent run-long gains. Climb the 120-floor Adventure tower toward The Origin, or unlock the endless Eternal Climb mode and draft your own difficulty: every two floors, the shrine offers three permanent curses you must choose between. Deeper climbs and heavier curses pay out more Ancient Coins for a dedicated meta-progression tree. Fourteen classes, 120 floors, 12 guardians, Game Center leaderboards, full offline play, and a single one-time purchase to unlock everything beyond floor 10. Around 200 enemies and 40 item sets across the climb.

핵심 특징

  • Three-way loot decisions — equip, absorb, or sacrifice every drop
  • Two campaign modes — 120-floor Adventure + endless Eternal Climb
  • You set the difficulty — draft permanent curses every two floors
  • 14 unlockable classes — Knight, Rogue, Mage, Paladin, Vampire, Necromancer, Berserker, Alchemist, Beastmaster, Monk, Ranger, Chronomancer, Ronin, Stormcaller
  • 10 unique two-phase bosses — the Wardens of the Endless Cycle, in shuffled order
  • Pixel art, dark fantasy — original art by the solo dev
  • One-handed portrait play — built for the phone, not ported to it
  • Free to try, one-time unlock — no subscriptions, no loot boxes, no ads
  • 10-language localization — EN, FR, DE, IT, JA, KO, PT, RU, ZH-Hans, ES
  • Full offline play + iCloud save sync via CloudKit
  • Game Center — 100+ achievements, 80+ leaderboards
  • Ghost Challenges — shareable run codes, head-to-head ghost replays
