PRIVACY POLICY

EFFECTIVE: JULY 29, 2026

The short version: LootShrine does not collect, store, or sell your personal data. There are no ads and no third-party trackers harvesting your information. We use privacy-focused analytics (TelemetryDeck) that collect no personal data, and RevenueCat to manage your purchase. Your game data stays on your device and in your iCloud account.

1. WHAT WE COLLECT

LootShrine does not collect any personal information. We do not require account creation, login credentials, email addresses, or any form of registration to play the game.

We collect anonymous, aggregated usage analytics through TelemetryDeck to understand how the game is used (e.g. which floors players reach, which classes are popular). This data contains no personal identifiers, no device IDs, no IP addresses, and cannot be traced back to any individual user. TelemetryDeck is designed to be privacy-first and is GDPR-compliant by default.

2. GAME DATA & STORAGE

All game progress, save data, and settings are stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, your save data is stored in your personal iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit framework. This data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

We do not have access to your iCloud data. We cannot read, modify, or delete your cloud saves.

3. GAME CENTER

LootShrine uses Apple's Game Center for leaderboards, achievements, and friend challenges. Game Center data (your player name, scores, and achievements) is managed entirely by Apple. We access your Game Center friends list solely to enable Ghost Challenge invitations between friends.

We do not store, transmit, or share your Game Center data with any third party.

4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

LootShrine uses the following third-party services:

  • TelemetryDeck — Privacy-focused product analytics. Collects anonymous, aggregated signals with no personal identifiers, no tracking across apps, and no user profiling. See TelemetryDeck's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — Manages all in-app purchases and receipt validation, including the one-time full-game unlock and the optional Supporter Packs. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts to verify your transactions. It does not collect your name, email, or any personal information. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Game Center — Leaderboards, achievements, and friend challenges (managed entirely by Apple).
  • Apple CloudKit — iCloud save sync (managed entirely by Apple).

LootShrine does not use any advertising SDKs, social media trackers, data brokers, or marketing platforms. No ads are shown, ever.

5. IN-APP PURCHASES

LootShrine is free to download. The first 10 floors can be played without any payment. Unlocking the full game (floors 11–120) is a one-time in-app purchase.

There are also optional Supporter Packs — cosmetic-only purchases that grant supporter portraits, frames, and nameplates. They never affect gameplay, stats, or balance. Buying them is entirely optional and never required to play any part of the game.

Beyond the full-game unlock and the optional Supporter Packs, there are no other in-app purchases — no consumables, no subscriptions, no loot boxes, and no advertising.

All purchase transactions are processed by the App Store and verified through RevenueCat using an anonymous identifier. No payment information is collected or stored by LootShrine.

Refund requests. If you ask Apple for a refund, Apple may ask us for information to help it decide. With your consent, RevenueCat responds on our behalf with a minimal set of facts: whether the purchase was successfully delivered and is working, that a free trial of the content was available before purchase, and our stated preference — which is to let Apple decide. No gameplay data, playtime, or personal information is shared. This happens only if you initiate a refund request.

6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

LootShrine does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or adults. Since no personal data is collected from any user, the app is compliant with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and similar regulations.

7. DATA SHARING

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share any personal data with third parties. Anonymous analytics signals are processed by TelemetryDeck, and anonymous purchase receipts are processed by RevenueCat, as described above. Neither service receives any personally identifiable information.

The one exception is refund handling: if you request a refund, the limited purchase-delivery facts described in Section 5 are shared with Apple to help resolve your request.

8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If we update this Privacy Policy, the changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Since LootShrine collects no data, significant changes are unlikely.
