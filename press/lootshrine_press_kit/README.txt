every item is a three-way decision — and you set the difficulty yourself.

PITCH (short, ~50 words)
------------------------
A solo-developed pixel art roguelite for iPhone and iPad. Climb a 120-floor
tower of dark fantasy where every item can be equipped, absorbed for a
temporary buff, or sacrificed at the shrine for permanent power. Free to try
for 10 floors; one purchase unlocks the rest. No ads, no subscriptions, no
pay-to-win.

PITCH (medium, ~130 words)
--------------------------
LootShrine is a turn-based roguelite RPG for iPhone and iPad, built solo by
Tim Pereverzev. The hook is the three-way loot decision: every item you pick
up can be equipped for stats, absorbed for a one-battle buff, or sacrificed at
the shrine for permanent run-long gains. Climb the 100-floor Adventure toward
the Origin and twenty more floors beyond it, or unlock the endless Eternal
Climb and draft your own difficulty: every two floors the shrine offers three
permanent curses, and another player's hero can climb at your side as an Echo.
Sixteen heroes, more than 200 enemies and 38 item sets in the base game, a
permanent Word to compose, full offline play — and one purchase unlocks
everything beyond floor 10. Kalzania: The Unspent Year is an optional paid
50-floor expansion with four more heroes.

KEY FEATURES
------------
- Three-way loot decisions: equip, absorb or sacrifice every drop
- Three ways to climb: the 100-floor Adventure plus 20 floors Beyond the
  Origin, the endless Eternal Climb, and the Kalzania expansion (paid)
- You set the difficulty: draft permanent curses every two floors
- 20 heroes, each with a signature skill and a 60-node skill tree
- Echoes: another player's hero climbs at your side in the Eternal Climb
- The Author's Word: a permanent meta layer of Fragments you compose
- A boss every 10 floors, each with its own cinematic and a second phase
- Built for iPhone and iPad: one-handed portrait play on the phone, a full
  layout on iPad, in portrait and landscape
- Free to try, one-time unlock: no subscriptions, no loot boxes, no ads
- 17 languages · full offline play · iCloud save sync
- Game Center leaderboards, 160+ achievements, Ghost Challenges and the
  Hall of the Fallen

KALZANIA: THE UNSPENT YEAR (DLC 1, paid, needs the full game)
--------------------------------------------------------------
A separate 50-floor campaign across ten regions, with 110 new enemies, ten
bosses, six Word Seals and four new heroes — the Chandler, Mason, Almoner and
Apparitor. King Corvain waits on floor 50. In the game: Main Menu → ADD-ONS.
