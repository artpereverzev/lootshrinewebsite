PITCH (medium ~120 words)
-------------------------
LootShrine is a turn-based, portrait-orientation roguelite RPG for iPhone and iPad,
built solo by Tim Pereverzev. The hook is the three-way loot decision: every item
you pick up can be equipped for stats, absorbed for a one-battle buff, or sacrificed
at the shrine for permanent run-long gains. Climb the 100-floor Adventure tower
toward The Origin, or unlock the endless Eternal Climb mode and draft your own
difficulty: every two floors, the shrine offers three permanent curses you must
choose between. Deeper climbs and heavier curses pay out more Ancient Coins for a
dedicated meta-progression tree. Eight classes, 10 unique two-phase Wardens, Game
Center leaderboards, full offline play, and a single one-time purchase to unlock
everything beyond floor 10.

KEY FEATURES
------------
- Three-way loot decisions (equip / absorb / sacrifice)
- Two campaign modes: 100-floor Adventure + endless Eternal Climb
- You draft permanent curses every two floors in Eternal Climb
- 8 unlockable classes (Knight, Rogue, Mage, Paladin, Vampire, Necromancer,
  Berserker, Alchemist)
- 10 unique two-phase bosses — the Wardens of the Endless Cycle, shuffled
- Pixel art, dark fantasy aesthetic — all art by the solo dev
- One-handed portrait play — built for the phone, not ported to it
- Free to try, one-time unlock — no subscriptions, no loot boxes, no ads
- 5-language localization
- Full offline play + iCloud save sync via CloudKit
- Game Center: 75+ achievements, 72+ leaderboards
- Ghost Challenges — shareable run codes, ghost replays
