PITCH — COPY ANY OF THESE

Drop-in copy for articles. Verified accurate; use freely.

ONE-LINER

LootShrine is a free-to-try pixel art roguelite RPG for iOS where every item is a three-way decision — and you set the difficulty yourself.

SHORT (~50 WORDS)

A solo-developed pixel art roguelite for iOS. Climb a 100-floor tower of dark fantasy where every item can be equipped, absorbed for a temporary buff, or sacrificed at the shrine for permanent power. Free to try for 10 floors; one purchase unlocks the rest. No ads, no subscriptions, no pay-to-win.

MEDIUM (~120 WORDS)

LootShrine is a turn-based, portrait-orientation roguelite RPG for iPhone and iPad, built solo by Tim Pereverzev. The hook is the three-way loot decision: every item you pick up can be equipped for stats, absorbed for a one-battle buff, or sacrificed at the shrine for permanent run-long gains. Climb the 100-floor Adventure tower toward The Origin, or unlock the endless Eternal Climb mode and draft your own difficulty: every two floors, the shrine offers three permanent curses you must choose between. Deeper climbs and heavier curses pay out more Ancient Coins for a dedicated meta-progression tree. Eight classes, 10 unique two-phase Wardens, Game Center leaderboards, full offline play, and a single one-time purchase to unlock everything beyond floor 10.

KEY FEATURES

  • Three-way loot decisions — equip, absorb, or sacrifice every drop
  • Two campaign modes — 100-floor Adventure + endless Eternal Climb
  • You set the difficulty — draft permanent curses every two floors
  • 8 unlockable classes — Knight, Rogue, Mage, Paladin, Vampire, Necromancer, Berserker, Alchemist
  • 10 unique two-phase bosses — the Wardens of the Endless Cycle, in shuffled order
  • Pixel art, dark fantasy — original art by the solo dev
  • One-handed portrait play — built for the phone, not ported to it
  • Free to try, one-time unlock — no subscriptions, no loot boxes, no ads
  • 5-language localization — EN, RU, ZH-Hans, FR, ES
  • Full offline play + iCloud save sync via CloudKit
  • Game Center — 75+ achievements, 72+ leaderboards
  • Ghost Challenges — shareable run codes, head-to-head ghost replays
